# Third-party assets

The five hiragana stroke diagrams in `public/kana/` and the derived stroke paths
in `src/content/ingestion/kana-strokes.json` come from
[KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/), by Ulrich Apel and contributors.
Source: [KanjiVG repository](https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg/tree/master/kanji).

They are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
The SVG files retain their original notices. Atlas extracts their paths for an
ordered reveal and adds guides and stroke numbers in the printable vowel sheet.
Those diagram adaptations are available under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.
No endorsement by the authors is implied.

Teacher handouts, classroom photos, textbook pages, returned work and student
laboratory reports are private evidence. Atlas publishes original companions,
reference metadata and teacher links. It does not redistribute these sources.
