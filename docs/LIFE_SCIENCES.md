## Interaction

Assignment questions offer the requested meaning, a small explanation, a direct
main-idea link and what the student’s paper answer should include. No quiz,
rating, completion or answer-entry control is added. NOVA uses a compact list
suited to observing video facts rather than a tutoring stepper. Key ideas use
short collapsible explanations, original schematic visuals and direct backlinks.
Definitions review is an optional reveal-and-recall surface.

Product events contain only the canonical concept/material IDs and feature name.
They never include question wording, student responses or private source content.
